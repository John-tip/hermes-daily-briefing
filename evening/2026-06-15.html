AI fluency drill — follow-up

Concept: Tool-calling agents

A useful business agent is not just a chatbot. It can choose tools, read source data, take bounded actions, and report evidence. This is the bridge from AI answers to repeatable workflows.

Drill: take one real workflow and write: trigger → data sources → allowed tools → forbidden actions → human approval point → evidence returned → success metric → rollback/exception path.
