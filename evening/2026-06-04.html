AI fluency drill — follow-up

Morning concept: tool-calling agents — not chatbots, but systems that can select tools, inspect data, take bounded actions, and report evidence.

Tonight’s check: the useful version is boring and operational. Pick one workflow where an agent can save 20–30 minutes without being trusted to make irreversible decisions.

  • Candidate workflow: daily account-prep agent for sales calls.
  • Trigger: morning run or meeting prep request.
  • Data sources: CRM notes, dealer website, inventory/pricing signals, previous outreach, current product priorities.
  • Allowed tools: read-only CRM lookup, web research, summarization, draft next-best action.
  • Forbidden actions: sending messages, changing CRM records, committing discounts, or updating external systems without human approval.
  • Success metric: faster prep, better objection handling, cleaner next step, and fewer follow-ups that die in notes.
