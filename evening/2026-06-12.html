AI fluency drill — follow-up

Morning concept: Tool-calling agents. Tonight’s extension: tool-calling becomes business value only when paired with permissions, evidence, and rollback.

A useful business agent is not just a chatbot. It can choose tools, read source data, take bounded actions, and report evidence. This is the bridge from AI answers to repeatable workflows.

Drill: take one real workflow and write: trigger → data sources → allowed tools → forbidden actions → approval point → evidence returned → success metric → rollback/exception path. If the agent can spend money, contact a customer, change pricing, or alter CRM/DMS data, add a human gate.
  • Best candidate: dealer/account prep or post-call follow-up. Let the agent gather facts, draft next actions, and cite evidence. Do not let it send messages or mutate systems without approval.
  • Quality bar: saves 20–30 minutes, improves specificity, and makes stale next steps visible.
  • Business test: if the workflow cannot improve close rate, follow-through, manager visibility, or cycle time, it is probably entertainment—not leverage.
