AI fluency drill — follow-up

Morning concept: tool-calling agents — systems that can choose tools, inspect source data, take bounded actions, and report evidence rather than just producing chat answers.

Tonight’s check: can you design one boring, bounded workflow that saves 20–30 minutes without giving the agent irreversible authority?

  • Workflow candidate: daily sales/account prep agent.
  • Trigger: morning run, meeting-prep request, or account-review queue.
  • Data sources: CRM notes, dealer website, inventory/pricing signals, previous outreach, current product priorities, and approved internal playbooks.
  • Allowed tools: read-only lookup, web research, summarization, draft next-best action, and evidence report.
  • Forbidden actions: sending external messages, changing CRM records, committing discounts, changing customer data, or updating external systems without approval.
  • Success metric: faster prep, sharper objections, more specific follow-up, and fewer stale next steps buried in notes.
