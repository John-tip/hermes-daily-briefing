AI fluency drill — follow-up

Morning concept: Tool-calling agents.

A useful business agent is not just a chatbot. It can choose tools, read source data, take bounded actions, and report evidence. The practical jump is from “answer generator” to repeatable workflow with controls.

Tonight’s artifact: trigger → data sources → allowed tools → forbidden actions → human approval point → evidence returned → success metric → exception path.
  • Best first workflow: dealer/account prep or stale-opportunity cleanup. These are bounded, measurable, and close enough to revenue to matter.
  • Quality bar: the workflow must change a rep action, manager decision, customer conversation, or operating metric. If it only produces a prettier summary, it is too soft.
  • Approval point: any external message, CRM mutation, pricing recommendation, or customer-facing claim needs human approval until the evidence trail is strong.
